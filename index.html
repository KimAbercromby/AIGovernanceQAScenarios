AI Governance QA Scenarios

Every variation of system, use, money, risk, agency, Fast Track and screening that the suite has to handle. Expected results are written from the suite's own rules, with the clause cited, and the real triage engine is run against them in your browser. Where a rule depends on a person or a workbook formula rather than code, the test says so and shows the evidence.

Loading the triage engine…

1 · Rules first

Each scenario's expected result comes from a cited clause (Gate Map, Playbook, Fast-Track form), never from the engine's own output. A failure means the engine and the rule disagree.

2 · Three kinds of test

Engine scenarios run live against the triage engine. Workbook scenarios recalculate the Gate Log's row-check formulas. Human control scenarios list the steps a person must take.

3 · Tests that bite

Mutation testing puts realistic bugs back into a copy of the engine, including the one found in the Copilot simulation, and confirms at least one scenario fails each time.

4 · Decisions surfaced

Where the rules give an answer that may not be wanted, or leave a gap, the scenario is flagged for a policy decision rather than quietly passed.
